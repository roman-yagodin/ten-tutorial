
---------------------Tomb Raider Anniversary Revamped Egypt Textures by LoreRaider---------------------


Here's a pack which contains most of the Egypt textures used in the Tomb Raider Anniversary game, revamped (or recoloured)

Why you may wondering?
Because I've always thought these textures has potential, but their biggest flaw is the colour, they look really, really grey (even in the original game)
So, here you go!

This pack contains:
-TRAPack.png for TombEditor
-TRAPack.tga for NGLE

The TGA contains more than 32 pages, if you have troubles opening it with TBuilder,
you may want to use the Revised version which can be found at trsearch.org, it's called TBuilder2.1 R1
